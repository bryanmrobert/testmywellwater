Loudoun County, VA well water testing: VCE Loudoun program

Short answer: Join the waitlist for Loudoun County's Household Water Quality Testing Program, run by Virginia Cooperative Extension Loudoun at 750 Miller Drive SE, Suite 131, Leesburg. A kit costs $70 and covers bacteria, nitrate, lead, arsenic and dozens more parameters. The August 2026 round has ended; 2027 programs take waitlist sign-ups.

What's tested

Bacteria (total coliform and E. coli), nitrate, sulfate, iron, manganese, lead, copper, arsenic, fluoride, pH, total dissolved solids, hardness, sodium, barium, chromium, cadmium, uranium, selenium and more. Samples are analyzed at Virginia Tech. [source]

Between rounds

VAHWQP's campus labs only take samples during scheduled clinics, so use a certified private lab from VAHWQP's list if you can't wait. [source] See our Virginia guide.
