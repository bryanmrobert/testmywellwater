Virginia well water testing: VAHWQP drinking water clinics

The Virginia Household Water Quality Program (VAHWQP) offers drinking water clinics in around 70 Virginia counties every year through local Virginia Cooperative Extension offices. [source]

How a clinic works

  1. Pick up a sample kit at a local event or during a pick-up period.
  2. On the collection day, collect your sample following the instructions and drop it off locally.
  3. Samples go to Virginia Tech for analysis.
  4. About 4–5 weeks after collection, results come back by email, mail or at a local meeting. [source]

Cost and what's tested

Analysis is $70 per kit and covers 28 chemical and bacteriological constituents, including total coliform and E. coli, nitrate, lead, arsenic, iron, manganese, pH, hardness and more. VAHWQP says comparable analysis at a private commercial lab would cost more than $300. Financial assistance may be available through SERCAP. [source]

No clinic scheduled in your county?

VAHWQP's campus labs only handle samples during scheduled clinics. Contact your local Extension office to say you're interested, or use a certified private lab from their list. [source]

Buying or selling a home in Virginia

VAHWQP says Virginia has no regulations requiring private water supplies to be tested at the time of sale, but testing is part of a standard contract. Total coliform and E. coli testing is standard practice, and lead and nitrate are also recommended and required by some lenders. Ask your lender what's required. [source]

Contact: wellwater@vt.edu, 540-231-9058. [source]

FAQ

How much does VAHWQP well testing cost?

VAHWQP lists analysis at $70 per kit, covering 28 chemical and bacteriological constituents.

Does Virginia require well testing when a house is sold?

According to VAHWQP, there are no Virginia regulations requiring testing at sale, but testing is part of a standard contract and some lenders require lead and nitrate tests.

How long do VAHWQP results take?

Results are returned about 4 to 5 weeks after sample collection.
