Hunterdon County, NJ well water testing

Short answer: Raritan Headwaters, a watershed nonprofit, offers well test kits year-round at its Flemington office, 5 Darts Mill Road, and its Bedminster office, with a $70 basic test for coliform, fecal confirmation and nitrates; call 908-234-1852 ext. 401. Hunterdon County's Health Department, 908-788-1351, answers well water testing questions.

Hunterdon County Department of Health

The county directs well water testing questions to its Health Department: 314 State Route 12, County Complex, Building #1, Flemington, NJ 08822; phone 908-788-1351. [source] Its well guide recommends testing once a year for bacteria, and for nitrate, arsenic and volatile organic compounds every five years. [source] After a flood reaches a private well, the county says it must be tested and disinfected once the water recedes. [source]
