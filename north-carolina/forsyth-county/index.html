Forsyth County, NC well water testing: county sampling prices

Short answer: Call Forsyth County Environmental Health at 336-703-3225 and request an existing-well inspection plus sampling. Complete the water sample request form with payment (checks to FCDPH). The well must meet construction rules first; any violations found must be fixed within 30 days before a sample is taken. Bacteria is $52 on the Service Invoice.

USGS's 2015 county estimate put Forsyth County's self-supplied population at about 86,600. USGS says the most common source of self-supplied water is a well. No separate official county-wide private-well count was found beyond that USGS figure. [source]

What and how often to test

  • Every year: total and fecal coliform bacteria, and check the wellhead for cracks or openings.
  • Every two years: heavy metals, nitrates, nitrites, lead and copper.
  • Every five years: pesticides and volatile organic compounds, or yearly if a known pesticide is applied nearby.
  • Also test after well repairs, flooding or a change in taste, color or odor, and test for nitrates if you're pregnant or have an infant at home. [source]

NC State Extension says the best time for yearly testing is spring or summer following a rainy period. [source] If you're pregnant or have an infant and nitrates show up, NCDHHS says not to drink the water or use it for baby formula; boiling doesn't remove nitrates. [source] See our nitrate guide and coliform guide.

New wells and permits

For a household, irrigation or business water-supply well, you need a Well Construction Permit Application, completed and signed by the property owner with the fee. The owner sets a site meeting with the department to issue the permit; having the certified well contractor there is recommended. After drilling, an environmental health specialist inspects grout placement and the wellhead. When the well has passed inspection and has power for the pump, call Environmental Health at 336-703-3225 to schedule the initial sample. The initial analysis is included in the drinking-water well construction permit fee ($489 on the Service Invoice). [source] [source] Statewide, new private wells must be tested within 30 days of completion. [source]

FAQ

Where can I get my well water tested in Forsyth County, North Carolina?

Call Forsyth County Environmental Health at 336-703-3225 and request an existing-well inspection plus sampling. Complete the water sample request form with payment (checks to FCDPH). The well must meet construction rules first; any violations found must be fixed within 30 days before a sample is taken. Bacteria is $52 on the Service Invoice.

How much does well water testing cost in Forsyth County, NC?

Forsyth County Environmental Health's Service Invoice lists bacteria at $52, fluoride at $54, inorganic at $102, nitrate/nitrite at $54, pesticide, petroleum or organic (VOA) at $121 each, uranium plus three metals at $103, and inorganic with nitrate at $108.

Can I get my existing well sampled in Forsyth County without an inspection?

No. The county says the existing well must meet applicable rules before a sample is taken, and the request form says a section representative must inspect the well for construction-standard compliance before any samples are collected. Violations found must be corrected within 30 days.

Does the new-well permit include the first water test in Forsyth County?

Yes. The well permitting page says the cost of the initial water sample analysis is included in the well construction permit fee. The Service Invoice lists the drinking water well construction permit at $489. Call 336-703-3225 after the well passes inspection and has power to schedule the sample.
