Indiana well water testing: state lab prices and certified labs

How do I test my well water in Indiana? Step 1: Order a kit online from the Indiana Department of Health laboratory through Access Indiana. Private citizens pay $10 each for bacteria, nitrate or fluoride and $20 for arsenic, lead and copper, plus shipping. Step 2: Collect the sample in the container sent to you and return it. Step 3: Test bacteria and nitrate every year. Or choose a lab from IDEM's certified drinking water lab list; find your local health department in the state directory.

The U.S. Geological Survey estimated that about 1.68 million people in Indiana supplied their own household water in 2015. USGS says the most common source of self-supplied water is a well. [source] [source]

Private well water isn't regulated by IDEM or the EPA, so well owners are responsible for their own water's safety, according to the Indiana Department of Health (IDOH). [source]

What to test and how often

  • Every year: bacteria and nitrate.
  • At least every five years, or after major maintenance: fluoride, arsenic, lead and copper. [source]

IDOH state lab prices for private citizens

Shipping is extra. Kits are ordered online through Access Indiana, and containers are sent after the order. [source]

How much does the Indiana state lab charge for well water tests?

IDOH lists $10 each for bacteria, nitrate or fluoride and $20 for arsenic, lead and copper for private citizens, not including shipping.

Where can I find an official list of certified labs for well water testing in Indiana?

IDEM publishes its list of Indiana certified drinking water laboratories at https://www.in.gov/idem/cleanwater/files/dw_cert_lab_in-state.pdf.

Which health department pages should I use for private well testing in Indiana?

The Indiana Department of Health covers private well testing at https://www.in.gov/health/eph/well-water-quality-and-testing/, and the state's directory of local health departments is at https://www.in.gov/localhealth/.
