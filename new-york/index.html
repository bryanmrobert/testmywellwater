New York well water testing: ELAP-certified labs and how often to test

The New York State Department of Health (NYSDOH) advises testing your well at least once a year for bacteria, and for other contaminants every 3–5 years. [source]

Use an ELAP-certified lab

NYSDOH points well owners to laboratories certified by its Environmental Laboratory Approval Program (ELAP) for potable water, or to your local health department for a list. [source] You can search certified labs with the NYSDOH ELAP lab search. [source]

Also test when…

  • after you disinfect and flush the well or do maintenance (E. coli and coliform)
  • your water looks, smells or tastes different
  • after floods, changes in land use, or concerns about local contaminants [source]

Free PFAS testing pilot (select counties)

If you use a private well in Dutchess, Orange, Putnam, Suffolk, Ulster or Westchester County, you may be eligible for free PFAS testing and treatment rebates through the NYSDOH pilot program. [source]

Suspected fuel or chemical contamination: contact your health department and the NYS DEC Spill Hotline at 800-457-7362. [source]
