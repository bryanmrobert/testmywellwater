Onondaga County, NY well water testing: county-approved labs

Short answer: Call a lab the Onondaga County Health Department lists as approved for drinking water bacteriology and nitrates: Certified Environmental Services in North Syracuse, 315-478-2374; Microbac in Cortland, 607-753-3403; or JLI Environmental Laboratories in Auburn, 315-253-4433. What and how often to test

The county health department recommends testing private well water at least once a year for total coliform and nitrate. [source] New York State's Department of Health adds testing for other contaminants every 3–5 years. [source]

The county notes that when total coliform is detected, follow-up testing is needed to see whether E. coli is present, and the lab will automatically run an E. coli test after a positive total coliform result. [source]

Local geology

The county warns that even a properly located and built well can be contaminated. Fractured shale gives little or no filtration, so contaminants can travel long distances quickly, which can be a particular problem near farming or septic systems. [source]

County contact

The Onondaga County Health Department doesn't regulate private wells, but its engineering staff can give you information about your well water: Bureau of Public Health Engineering, 315-435-6600, John H. Mulroy Civic Center, 421 Montgomery Street, Syracuse, NY 13202. [source]
