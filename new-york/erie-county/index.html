Erie County, NY well water testing: county public health lab

Short answer: Call the Erie County Public Health Laboratory, 503 Kensington Avenue, Buffalo, at 716-898-6100 for sample bottles. Its fee schedule lists total coliform at $18, nitrate at $35, and lead or arsenic at $25 each. Deliver bacteria samples by 2 p.m., Monday through Thursday. The county recommends testing for coliform and nitrate every year.

What the county recommends testing

The Erie County Department of Health recommends testing well water at least once a year for total coliform bacteria and nitrate. If a baby is expected, it suggests testing for nitrate and total coliform early in pregnancy and again during the baby's first six months. [source]

Other labs and help with results

You can use any New York State Department of Health-certified environmental laboratory; the county points to the ELAP list, which includes its own lab. The county says most labs mail results within a week or two. For help interpreting results, call the Division of Environmental Health at 716-961-6800. [source] Search certified labs with the NYSDOH ELAP lab search. [source]

If coliform is found

The county says to disinfect the well, then resample. If the repeat sample is still positive, it recommends permanent disinfection or a certified well driller to find and fix the source. [source] See our coliform guide.

Buying a home with a well

The Erie County Department of Health inspects drinking water wells for all house property transfers, and a bacteriological sample is taken. Dug wells must get filtration and disinfection or be replaced with a drilled well before a transfer certification is issued. [source] See our New York guide.
