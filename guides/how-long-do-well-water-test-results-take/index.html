How long do well water test results take?

Short answer: It depends on the lab. Penn State's lab says tests are generally complete within 10 to 14 business days after it receives the sample. Virginia's VAHWQP clinics return results about 4 to 5 weeks after sample collection, and Loudoun County says about six weeks after drop-off. Bacteria samples must reach Penn State within 30 hours.

Program or labTurnaround it publishes
Penn State AASL (mail-in, PA)Generally 10–14 business days after the lab receives the sample [source]
VAHWQP drinking water clinics (VA)About 4–5 weeks after sample collection [source]
VCE Loudoun program (VA)About six weeks after sample drop-off, by email [source]

Shipping deadlines matter for bacteria

Penn State must receive bacteria samples within 30 hours of sampling, and it can't accept bacteria samples on a Friday. [source] Clinic programs avoid shipping by having everyone collect and drop off on a set day. [source]

Need results fast?

For a home sale or a suspected problem, call a state-certified lab and ask its turnaround before you sample. The CDC says your county or health department should have a list of state-certified labs. [source]
