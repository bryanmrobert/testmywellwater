Well water smells like rotten eggs: causes and what to test

A rotten-egg taste or odor is caused by hydrogen sulfide gas (H₂S). Minnesota's Department of Health says it can be naturally occurring, produced by sulfur bacteria in groundwater, the well or plumbing, produced inside water heaters, or, rarely, from pollution. [source]

Hot water only, or hot and cold?

SymptomCommon cause (Penn State Extension)
Odor in hot water onlyReaction with the magnesium anode rod in the water heater. Replacing the rod may void the heater warranty.
Odor in hot and cold water, black greasy stainsHydrogen sulfide, sulfides or sulfate-reducing bacteria in groundwater
Odor at one sink onlyMay be bacteria in the drain rather than the water

[source]

What to test

MDH says that in most cases the smell isn't related to the sanitary quality of the water, but to be safe you should test your well water for coliform bacteria and nitrate. [source] The EPA lists hydrogen sulfide, corrosion and metals as tests for objectionable taste or smell. [source]

Safety note

MDH warns that hydrogen sulfide gas in the air can be harmful at high levels. Only well professionals with proper precautions should enter a well pit or enclosed space where the gas may be present. [source]
