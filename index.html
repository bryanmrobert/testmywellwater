Short answer: Start with your local health department, which the EPA says may test private wells for free, then a state-certified lab or a state program such as Virginia's $70 VAHWQP clinic kit. The EPA and CDC both say to test every year for total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH.

Private wells are the owner's responsibility. The EPA advises testing a private well every year for total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH, and notes your local health department may offer private well testing for free. [source]

FAQ

Where can I get my well water tested?

Start with your local health department, which the EPA says may test private wells for free, then a state-certified lab or a state program such as Virginia's $70 VAHWQP clinic kit. The EPA and CDC both say to test every year for total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH.
