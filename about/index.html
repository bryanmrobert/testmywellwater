About Test My Well Water

Short answer: Test My Well Water is an independent directory of private well water testing options, published by Robert Companies LLC in Austin, Texas. We summarize official sources, such as health departments, state programs and university labs, cite every fact, and show the date each page was last updated. We are not a laboratory.

Test My Well Water is an independent directory that helps private well owners find official, low-cost ways to test their water, state by state and eventually county by county. It's published by Robert Companies LLC.

How we research

  • Every program, fee, lab rule and law on this site comes from an official source (government agency, state program or university lab) that we read directly, and it's cited on the page.
  • Each page shows the date we last checked it. Programs and fees change, so always confirm with the source.
  • We don't accept payment to list programs. Some product links may earn a commission (see our affiliate disclosure).
