Lancaster County, PA well water testing

Short answer: Ask the Penn State Extension Lancaster County office, 1383 Arcadia Road, Room 140, Lancaster, 717-394-6851, open weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., about Penn State water test kits; packages cost $60 to $220. Chester County's lab list also names two Lancaster County labs: Eurofins Lancaster Laboratories and Owl Hill Laboratories.

Penn State Extension, Lancaster County office

1383 Arcadia Road, Room 140, Lancaster, PA 17601; phone 717-394-6851; office hours Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m. Penn State says to call ahead to make sure someone is available. [source]

Penn State says its drinking water test kits are available at many county extension offices [source], so call to ask whether this office has kits in stock.

Price and timing (Penn State lab)

Packages run from $60 (standard: total coliform, E. coli, pH, TDS) to $220 (extensive); Agriculture/Septic, which adds nitrate, is $70. Send by overnight mail; bacteria samples must arrive within 30 hours and can't be accepted on a Friday. Results generally take 10–14 business days. [source]
