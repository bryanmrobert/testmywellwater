Mail-in option: Penn State

Penn State's DEP-accredited lab offers packages from $60 (standard) to $220 (extensive). Kits are at many county extension offices. Bacteria samples must arrive within 30 hours, and the lab can't accept them on a Friday. [source] See our Pennsylvania guide.
