Maryland well water testing: what MDE recommends

How do I test my well water in Maryland? Step 1: Choose a lab from the Maryland Department of the Environment's list of state-certified drinking water laboratories. Step 2: Get its bottles and instructions and sample at least yearly for total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH, and whenever taste, odor or appearance changes. Step 3: Your local health department can help identify likely problems; call MDE's Water Well Construction Program at 410-537-3784 with questions.

The U.S. Geological Survey estimated that about 1.42 million people in Maryland supplied their own household water in 2015. USGS says the most common source of self-supplied water is a well. [source] [source]

About 350,000 Maryland households rely on private wells, and owners are responsible for the safety of their own drinking water, according to the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE). [source]

What and when to test

  • At minimum, sample annually for total coliform bacteria, nitrates, total dissolved solids and pH.
  • Sample whenever odor, taste or appearance changes, or you suspect contaminants.
  • More frequent testing may be warranted, for example with unexplained illness in the family, someone pregnant or nursing, or a neighbor finding a contaminant. [source]

Where to test

Use a state-certified laboratory that does drinking water testing. MDE publishes a list of certified labs. [source] [source] Local health departments, University of Maryland Extension, local university geologists and nearby public water systems can help identify likely problems. [source]

Contact: MDE Water Well Construction Program, 410-537-3784. [source]

Where can I find an official list of certified labs for well water testing in Maryland?

The Maryland Department of the Environment publishes its list of water quality laboratories certified in Maryland at https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/water/water_supply/Documents/MD-Certified-Drinking-Water-Lab-MAR2024.pdf.

Which health department pages should I use for private well testing in Maryland?

MDE's private well guidance is at https://mde.maryland.gov/programs/Water/Water_Supply/Pages/Be_Well_Wise.aspx, and the Maryland Department of Health lists every local health department at https://health.maryland.gov/Pages/health-departments.aspx.
