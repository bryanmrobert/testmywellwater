California domestic well testing: State Water Board guidance and ELAP labs

How do I test my well water in California? Step 1: Pick a lab from California's ELAP certified laboratories map; it supplies bottles and can help you sample. Step 2: Ask for a written estimate, since the State Water Board says basic sampling can cost $100 to $400. Step 3: Test every year and compare results to drinking water limits; your county environmental health department can help with the results. Rental wells in free testing program areas must use that program.

The U.S. Geological Survey estimated that about 1.44 million people in California supplied their own household water in 2015. USGS says the most common source of self-supplied water is a well. [source] [source]

Test every year

The State Water Board's GAMA program recommends testing domestic well water quality every year. If results are high, compare them to the Maximum Contaminant Levels set for public water systems. MCLs aren't enforceable for domestic wells, but the Board recommends you observe the same levels. [source]

What to test for

The Board lists bacteria (such as E. coli and total coliforms), inorganics (such as arsenic, uranium and total dissolved solids), fertilizers such as nitrate, pesticides, and industrial solvents. [source]

Where to test and cost

The Board suggests an Environmental Laboratory Accreditation Program (ELAP) lab. The lab supplies sampling bottles and can help you sample. Basic sampling can cost $100 to $400, so ask for a written estimate first. [source] Find labs on the California ELAP certified laboratories map. [source]

Renting out a home with a well (AB 2454)

If a domestic well serves a rental property inside the area of a free testing program funded or regulated by the State or a Regional Water Board, the owner must test through that free program and share results with tenants within 10 days. The owner can't charge tenants for testing. [source]

Where can I find an official list of certified labs for well water testing in California?

The California State Water Board's ELAP certified laboratories map is at https://waterboards.maps.arcgis.com/apps/webappviewer/index.html?id=bd0bd8b42b1944058244337bd2a4ebfa.

Which health department pages should I use for private well testing in California?

The State Water Board advises domestic well owners at https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/gama/domestic_wells_testing.html, and its Local Primacy Agency list gives the county environmental health office contacts at https://www.waterboards.ca.gov/drinking_water/programs/documents/web_contact_info_lpa.pdf.
